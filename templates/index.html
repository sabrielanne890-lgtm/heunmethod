Heun's Method

(Improved Euler Method)

Mathematical Background

Given an initial value problem:

\[ \frac{dy}{dx} = f(x, y), \quad y(x_0) = y_0 \]

Euler's method uses a single slope at the left endpoint. Heun's method improves accuracy by averaging two slopes.

Predictor:

\[ \tilde{y}_{i+1} = y_i + h \cdot f(x_i, y_i) \]

Corrector:

\[ y_{i+1} = y_i + \frac{h}{2} \left[ f(x_i, y_i) + f(x_{i+1}, \tilde{y}_{i+1}) \right] \]

Heun's Method is a second-order Runge-Kutta Method.

Example 1: dy/dx = x + y

Solve \[ \frac{dy}{dx} = x + y \] with \( y(0)=1 \) using \( h=0.1 \). Find \( y(0.2) \).

Step 1: \( x_0 = 0 \to x_1 = 0.1 \)

\( f(0,1) = 0 + 1 = 1 \)

Predictor: \( \tilde{y}_1 = 1 + 0.1 \cdot 1 = 1.1 \)

\( f(0.1, 1.1) = 0.1 + 1.1 = 1.2 \)

Corrector: \( y_1 = 1 + \frac{0.1}{2}(1 + 1.2) = 1 + 0.05 \cdot 2.2 = 1.11 \)

Step 2: \( x_1 = 0.1 \to x_2 = 0.2 \)

\( f(0.1, 1.11) = 0.1 + 1.11 = 1.21 \)

Predictor: \( \tilde{y}_2 = 1.11 + 0.1 \cdot 1.21 = 1.231 \)

\( f(0.2, 1.231) = 0.2 + 1.231 = 1.431 \)

Corrector: \( y_2 = 1.11 + \frac{0.1}{2}(1.21 + 1.431) = 1.11 + 0.05 \cdot 2.641 = 1.24205 \)

Result: \( y(0.2) \approx 1.24205 \)

Exact solution: \( y = 2e^x - x - 1 \) → \( y(0.2) \approx 1.24214 \). Error ≈ 0.00009.

Example 2: dy/dx = -2xy

Solve \[ \frac{dy}{dx} = -2xy \] with \( y(0)=1 \) using \( h=0.2 \). Find \( y(0.4) \).

Step 1: \( x_0 = 0 \to x_1 = 0.2 \)

\( f(0,1) = -2 \cdot 0 \cdot 1 = 0 \)

Predictor: \( \tilde{y}_1 = 1 + 0.2 \cdot 0 = 1.0 \)

\( f(0.2, 1.0) = -2 \cdot 0.2 \cdot 1.0 = -0.4 \)

Corrector: \( y_1 = 1 + \frac{0.2}{2}(0 + (-0.4)) = 1 + 0.1 \cdot (-0.4) = 0.96 \)

Step 2: \( x_1 = 0.2 \to x_2 = 0.4 \)

\( f(0.2, 0.96) = -2 \cdot 0.2 \cdot 0.96 = -0.384 \)

Predictor: \( \tilde{y}_2 = 0.96 + 0.2 \cdot (-0.384) = 0.8832 \)

\( f(0.4, 0.8832) = -2 \cdot 0.4 \cdot 0.8832 = -0.70656 \)

Corrector: \( y_2 = 0.96 + \frac{0.2}{2}(-0.384 + (-0.70656)) = 0.96 + 0.1 \cdot (-1.09056) = 0.850944 \)

Result: \( y(0.4) \approx 0.850944 \)

Exact solution: \( y = e^{-x^2} \) → \( y(0.4) = e^{-0.16} \approx 0.85214 \). Error ≈ 0.0012.
